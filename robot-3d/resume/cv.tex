\cventry{}{CRS Arm Task-Space Impedance Controller}{C, TMS320F28335, Control Theory}{}{}{Task-space PD impedance control on a three-link arm, mapped to joints through the Jacobian transpose and closed at 1 kHz on a timer interrupt. Contact tasks use a feedforward downward force commanded through the motor torque constant, with no force sensor and no closed-loop force feedback, validated afterward against a physical scale. Written in C on a TI TMS320F28335. Peg insertion, zig-zag tracking, and pushing an egg without breaking it.}
